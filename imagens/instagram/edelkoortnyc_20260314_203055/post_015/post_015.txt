Meet the speaker: 
Mahenaz Chowdury is a cultural researcher, curator and conscious designer known for her decolonial approach to craft and cultural preservation. Founder of Broqué and workshop lead at Dhaka Makers, she uses artisanal knowledge as a tool for collaboration and social change. Her projects — including Once There Were Rivers, Loomscape and Weaving Continuity — explore Bangladeshi craft, Jamdani textiles and community resilience in the face of displacement, urbanisation and climate change.
