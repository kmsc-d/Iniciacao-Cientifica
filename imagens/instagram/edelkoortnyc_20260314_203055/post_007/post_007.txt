Tomorrow, Saturday, Feb 21st we will learn from Njoki Karu — singer-songwriter, performer, and certified Music Therapist (MT-BC) — as she shares insights from her artistic journey. Blending Folk, Soul, Gospel, and Traditional Kenyan music, Njoki creates deeply moving soundscapes that remind us our humanity is meant to be shared. Acclaimed within the Kenyan music scene, she is known for songs like Omba and her powerful reinterpretations of spiritual classics such as Binadamu and Bwana U Sehemu.
