Kevin Mwachiro will join World Hope Forum East Africa to talk about him as a Kenyan writer, journalist, podcaster, and queer activist with over two decades of experience in media and communications. He began his career at the BBC, contributing to radio, television, and digital platforms. A committed custodian of people’s stories, his work focuses on amplifying marginalised voices, particularly LGBTQI communities in Kenya and across Africa. He is the author of Invisible – Stories from Kenya’s Queer Community, founder of the Nipe Story podcast, and co-founder of the Out Film Festival, East Africa’s first LGBTQI film festival.
