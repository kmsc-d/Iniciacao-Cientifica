Meet the Speaker:
Natalie “Alabama” Chanin is an American fashion designer from Florence, Alabama and a pioneer of the zero-waste fashion movement. Through her brands Alabama Chanin and The School of Making, she works with organic cotton sourced sustainably from seed to fabric, collaborating with artisans to create garments by hand. Guided by an open-source philosophy, Chanin shares patterns and techniques through books and workshops, fostering a community rooted in craft, transparency, and care for people and planet. Her work celebrates the beauty of garments made slowly, designed to last and evolve with the life of the wearer.
