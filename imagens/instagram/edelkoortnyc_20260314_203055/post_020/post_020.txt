Haberdashery is back at the heart of fashion. From buttons and trims to pleats, pins and hardware, these once-overlooked details now define heritage, identity and brand handwriting. Blending tradition with play, romance and rebellion, haberdashery drives a new era of dressing—where craft, emotion and ornament bring charm, colour and storytelling back to clothes.
